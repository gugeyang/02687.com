Move beyond flat relational databases. By integrating a Graph Database (like Neo4j) into your educational infrastructure, you can map the complex relationships between students, curriculum concepts, and assessment outcomes.

### Overview
This solution helps universities map prerequisite dependencies dynamically, enabling truly personalized learning paths that automatically suggest prerequisite reviews when a student struggles with an advanced concept.

### Key Benefits
* **Personalized Learning:** Automated remedial content suggestions based on graph traversal.
* **Curriculum Auditing:** Easily spot disconnected courses or redundant materials across different departments.
* **Deep Analytics:** Perform community detection to understand how different student cohorts progress.
