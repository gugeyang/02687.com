## About 02687.com

**02687.com** is an open EdTech infrastructure platform and practitioner knowledge base. We document real-world architectures, deployment strategies, and engineering decisions for building modern, self-hosted educational technology — from Moodle LMS deployments to serverless learning analytics pipelines.

We are a collective of EdTech architects, developers, and educators focused on a critical operational question: *How do universities and independent educators build resilient, cost-effective digital learning infrastructure without being locked into expensive SaaS monopolies?*

### What the 02687 Platform Covers

The 02687.com knowledge base is organized across four technical domains:

- **Smart Infrastructure**: Production-grade Docker Compose blueprints, Nginx reverse proxy configurations, Kubernetes orchestration patterns, and HomeLab self-hosting strategies for campus LMS deployments.
- **Data & AI**: Knowledge Graph construction with Neo4j for curriculum mapping, xAPI + Learning Record Store (LRS) architectures to replace legacy SCORM systems, and Python ETL pipelines for migrating Student Information System data.
- **Security & Identity**: SAML 2.0 SSO integration with Azure AD for Moodle, Keycloak campus-wide identity federation, certificate management, and IAM hardening for educational platforms.
- **Developer Logs**: Hands-on Python automation scripts for Canvas LMS REST APIs, serverless plagiarism detection pipelines on AWS Lambda, containerized auto-grading systems, and Prometheus + Grafana monitoring stacks tuned for LMS workloads.

### Who We Write For

The primary audience of 02687.com includes university IT administrators managing campus LMS infrastructure, EdTech developers building integrations and automation workflows, and independent educators who prefer owning their infrastructure over paying per-seat SaaS licensing fees.

Every article published on 02687.com documents a configuration that has been deployed and tested in real environments, with the failure modes and trade-offs included alongside the working configurations.

### Our Technical Philosophy

We favor containers over bare-metal installations, open-source platforms where operational maturity permits, and observability-first deployments — no production stack goes live without monitoring. Student learning data sovereignty is a core principle: where possible, we document architectures that keep institutional data under institutional control rather than in third-party analytics clouds.
