## How We Review and Compare LMS Platforms

Most "best LMS" lists online are affiliate pages ranked by who pays the highest commission, not by what actually works. We built 02687.com's review process to be the opposite. This page explains exactly how we evaluate the platforms in our buyer's guides, so you can judge our recommendations for yourself.

### Our Evaluation Criteria

Every learning management system we cover is assessed against the same seven criteria. These are the factors that, in our hands-on experience, actually determine whether a deployment succeeds or becomes a costly mistake:

1. **Fit for the stated use case** — A platform that is excellent for university teaching can be wrong for corporate compliance training. We score each tool against the *specific* scenario the guide is about, not in the abstract.
2. **Total cost of ownership** — Beyond the sticker price: per-user scaling, implementation, hosting (for self-hosted options), customization, and ongoing maintenance.
3. **Deployment and administration effort** — How much technical skill and ongoing work it takes to run the system in production.
4. **Customization and extensibility** — Plugins, APIs, theming, and integration with HRIS/CRM/SIS systems.
5. **Scalability** — Whether the platform holds up from dozens of users to tens of thousands.
6. **Core learning features** — Course authoring, assessments, SCORM/xAPI support, reporting, and learner experience.
7. **Support and ecosystem** — Vendor support quality, community size, and documentation depth.

### Where Our Data Comes From

Our reviews combine three sources, and we are explicit about which is which:

- **Hands-on deployment experience.** Our team has deployed, migrated, and administered open-source and commercial LMS platforms in real production environments — including Moodle, Open edX, Canvas, Chamilo, and Sakai. Where we have run a platform ourselves, we say so.
- **Current vendor data.** Pricing models, plan tiers, and feature lists are checked against vendors' own published information at the time of writing. Pricing changes often — we date our guides and tell you to confirm current pricing on the vendor's site before buying.
- **Aggregated user reviews.** We reference public ratings from platforms like G2 and Capterra to reflect the experience of a wider user base than any single team could represent.

### Our Independence

- We are **not owned by, and do not take payment from, any LMS vendor** in exchange for favorable coverage or ranking.
- When a guide contains affiliate links, that relationship **never** changes our scoring or recommendations, and we disclose it.
- Every comparison includes **real weaknesses** for each product. A review that lists only strengths is marketing, not analysis — and we treat it as a failure.

### How Often We Update

The LMS market moves quickly: vendors change pricing, release features, and rebrand. We date every guide and revise our higher-traffic comparisons as the underlying facts change. If you spot something out of date, email us at **admin@02687.com** and we'll verify and correct it.
